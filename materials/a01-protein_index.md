## Vitamin D Receptor (VDR)

- **UniProt ID:** [P11473](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P11473)
- **PDB link:** [RCSB PDB](https://www.rcsb.org/groups/summary/polymer_entity/P11473)

**Description:**

A nuclear receptor that binds **vitamin D3 (calcitriol)** and regulates genes controlling calcium homeostasis and bone metabolism.
Functions as a **ligand-activated transcription factor**, forming heterodimers with the **retinoid X receptor (RXR)**.

**Used to demonstrate:**

- Database search and structure retrieval

## p53 Tumour Suppressor

- **UniProt ID:** [P04637](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P04637)
- **PDB link:** [RCSB PDB](https://www.rcsb.org/groups/summary/polymer_entity/P04637)

**Description:**

A key **tumour suppressor** and **transcription factor** that regulates cell cycle arrest, DNA repair, and apoptosis in response to stress or DNA damage.
Functions as a **homotetramer**, binding DNA and activating repair genes.

**Used to demonstrate:**

- Monomer prediction
- Heterodimer prediction with MDM2
- Homotetramer bound to DNA

## MDM2 (E3 Ubiquitin Ligase)

- **UniProt ID:** [Q00987](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/Q00987)
- **PDB link:** [RCSB PDB](https://www.rcsb.org/groups/summary/polymer_entity/Q00987)

**Description:**

An **E3 ubiquitin ligase** that binds to p53’s transactivation domain and promotes its **proteasomal degradation**, acting as a negative regulator of p53.
Plays a central role in balancing cell growth and apoptosis, and is a key target in cancer research.

**Used to demonstrate:**

- Heterodimer prediction with p53

## WhiB6

- **UniProt ID:** [P9WF37](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P9WF37)
- **PDB link:** [8D5V](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/8D5V) (heterodimer with SigmaA-R4–RNAP Beta-Flap Tip Chimera)

**Description:**

A small **redox-sensing transcriptional regulator** from *Mycobacterium tuberculosis*.
Part of the WhiB family (WhiB1–WhiB7).
Binds a **[4Fe–4S] iron–sulfur cluster**, interacts with RNA polymerase and sigma factors, and may modulate gene expression in response to redox state.

**Used to demonstrate:**

- Monomer prediction
- Heterodimer prediction

## Bacteriophage PA1c gp2 (ChmB-like protein)

- **UniParc ID:** [UPI0010C4F104](https://www.uniprot.org/uniparc/UPI0010C4F104)
- **PDB link:** [7UYX](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/7UYX) (homodimer)

**Description:**

A **nuclear shell-associated protein** from a jumbo bacteriophage.
Interacts with the major shell protein **ChmA**, likely forming **pores** that enable RNA and protein translocation and capsid docking.
Part of a protective "phage nucleus" that shields viral DNA from host defences.

**Used to demonstrate:**

- Monomer prediction
- Homodimer prediction

## Estrogen Receptor (ERα)

- **UniProt ID:** [P03372](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P03372)
- **PDB link:** [1ERE](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/1ERE) (heterodimer with 17β-estradiol)

**Description:**

A **nuclear hormone receptor** that binds **17β-estradiol** and regulates gene expression in reproductive and other tissues.
Ligand binding induces conformational changes that enable **DNA binding** and **coactivator recruitment**.

**Used to demonstrate:**

- Visualisation and analysis in ChimeraX
