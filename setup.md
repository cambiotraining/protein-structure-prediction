## Software

### Google account

Using **AlphaFold Server** requires a Google account.
You can use either a **personal or institutional** Google account.

- Open <a href="https://alphafoldserver.com/" target="_blank">AlphaFold Server</a> and login using your Google account.

### ChimeraX

ChimeraX is available on all major operating systems. 
Note that this software is only free for for **academic, government, nonprofit, and personal use**. 
Consult the ChimeraX page for other uses.

- Download the installer for your operating system from <a href="https://www.cgl.ucsf.edu/chimerax/download.html" target="_blank">the ChimeraX download page</a>
